## Start Here

1. Read [requirements and author decisions](requirements.md).
2. Consult the complete [submission guide snapshot](official_pages/submission-guidelines.html), especially its article-type table and first/revised submission sections.

4. Recheck the live official pages before submitting; requirements can change.

## Provenance and Limits

- [Source registry](sources.json) identifies all nine downloads and their official URLs.
- [Download manifest](download_manifest.json) records retrieval timestamps, HTTP status, sizes, content types and SHA-256 checksums.
- HTML files are unmodified response bodies. They retain publisher navigation and scripts; styles, images and other dependencies were not bundled. They are reference snapshots, not a fully offline website. Use the live links for navigation and submission.
- Official files retain their original rights. Collection for reference does not grant permission to redistribute publisher materials with the TE-KG dataset.
- The notes in [requirements.md](requirements.md) are AI-prepared summaries, not official instructions or manuscript prose. Full official pages take precedence.
- No journal account was accessed, no submission was started, and no form was completed or signed.
